# The script

Narration only, no stage directions. About 2.6 spoken words a second:
| Length | Words |
|---|---|
| 15 s | ~38 |
| 20 s | ~52 |
| 30 s | ~78 |

## The shape
1. **Hook question** (sentence 1, no setup): "Why do onions make you cry?" / "What if you fell into a black hole?"
2. **The mechanism in 2-3 visual steps**, each one something you can draw: "Slice one, and you burst tiny cells inside." "They release a sneaky gas that floats straight up into your eyes."
3. **A funny turn**: the body, the object or nature reacts like a person. "Your eyes panic, and flood it with tears."
4. **Payoff**: short, a joke that answers the hook, the last words of the video. "So you're not sad. The onion is *attacking* you."

## Style
- Spoken English: contractions, "you", sentences of 3-14 words. Numbers as words so the timing matches the voice.
- Every sentence must be drawable. Cut lines that only explain.
- "…" where the narrator should pause before a punchline: that pause is where the picture does its joke.
- `*word*` marks a caption word shown in yellow (the voice ignores the stars).
- Facts must be right. Hedge theories ("scientists think").

## Reply with
- The script in a copyable block, its word count and the estimated length.
- One line per planned shot.
- Voice tips: one warm, curious narrator. Your own voice on a phone in a quiet room works great. ElevenLabs: a storyteller voice, stability ~0.4-0.5; audio tags like [curious], [chuckles], [pause] make it sound human. No music in the voice file.
