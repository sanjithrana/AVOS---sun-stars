# Question ideas (each one is visual, a little weird, and ends on a joke)

Body: Why do onions make you cry? Why does your stomach growl? Why do we yawn? Why do fingers wrinkle in water? What if you never slept? Why do we get brain freeze? Why do we hiccup? Why do feet fall asleep?
Space: What if you fell into a black hole? What if the Moon disappeared? Why is space dark when stars are everywhere? What if you sneezed in space? Why don't astronauts cry properly in space?
Nature: Why is the ocean salty? Why do cats always land on their feet? Why is the sky blue? Why do leaves change colour? Why do bees make honey and not jam? Where does rain come from?
Everyday: Why does popcorn pop? Why does ice float? Why do mirrors flip left and right but not up and down? Why does bread go stale? Why is yawning contagious?
What if: What if you dug through the Earth? What if everyone jumped at once? What if you only ate sugar for a week? What if humans had tails?

Offer 10 as a numbered list (a one-line hook for each) and ask for a number or "more".
