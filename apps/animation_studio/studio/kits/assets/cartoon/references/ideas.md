# 10 story ideas that stop the scroll

Give exactly 10, numbered, in one reply. Then ask: "Reply with a number (or 'more')."

## Each idea
- **Title** (5-8 words, makes people curious): "The Day the Mona Lisa Vanished".
- **Hook line**: the first spoken sentence, a surprising fact said plainly: "Two empires almost went to war… over one pig."
- **The twist**: the ironic last line the whole video builds to.
- **Why it animates well**: 2-3 big visual moments (a chase, a collapse, a reveal, a crowd, a transformation).
- **True or legend**: documented history, a disputed account, or folklore. Say it plainly.

## What makes a great idea for this format
- One story with a clear cause and effect you can SHOW: someone does something, something big happens.
- An everyman main character, stakes that grow, and a twist in the last line.
- A world you can build from simple 3D shapes plus cartoon characters: towns, farms, islands, ships, palaces, museums, deserts, labs, castles, trains, bridges.
- Animals and objects with personality are gold (an emu army, a famous painting, a pig, a cat that runs a station).
- Mix the 10 across eras and themes: ancient, medieval, 1800s, 1900s, modern; science, animals, inventions, disasters, weird laws, lucky accidents, heists, wars nobody fought.
- If you present something as true, check the key facts with a web search first when you can. If it's a viral legend, frame it as "legend says".
- Nothing graphic, nothing political, and no real living people as the joke.

## Example (format only, don't reuse)
**1. The Great Emu War**
- Hook: "In 1932, Australia went to war… with birds."
- Twist: the army gives up and the emus keep the wheat.
- Animates well: an army truck rolling in, 20,000 emus scattering, an emu in a tiny helmet.
- Documented history (Western Australia, 1932).
